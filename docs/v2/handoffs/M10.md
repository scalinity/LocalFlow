## Reusable patterns for M11–M13

- **`_finalized_policy` is the template for any future finalize-time rebuild:** one helper owns the merged policy; the M06 scope upgrade and the M10 upgrade both route through it, so "one consumer drops another's half" is structurally closed.
- **Layer-3 additions:** register new intent grammars in `ALL_SYNTAX_GRAMMARS`, add their class to `COMMAND_CLASSES` (quote-zone blocking), and freeze their registries on the engine `ContextSnapshot` (duck-typed field, default None) — snippets and file tags are the worked example.
- **Frozen registries:** the m10 dict (`rules`, `wp`, `snippet_snapshot`, `skill_records` + rev, `skills`, `file_resolver`) is the per-job freeze pattern; caches key on (store-revision | path-mtime) and jobs keep their captured objects by value.
- **Honest fallbacks to copy:** non-executable modes, uncertified surfaces, absent categories — each records its reason where the user and the evidence both see it.
