# Annotation icon

Two equally weighted ivory annotation sheets on a muted teal rounded tile. The center notches express comparison; neither side is marked as the winner. No lettering, model names, or decorative badges.

Source artwork: `assets/app-icon.png`. Native icon family: `src-tauri/icons/icon.icns`; raster variants: `32x32.png`, `128x128.png`, `128x128@2x.png`, and `icon.png` in the same directory.

## Design prompt

Create a finished macOS desktop app icon for LocalFlow Annotate, a calm personal tool comparing two blinded versions of cleaned dictation. Asset only, centered, straight-on, square 1024x1024 composition. Native macOS rounded-square continuous-curvature tile with generous transparent margins outside its silhouette. Tile in deep muted teal #195c5d, gently lit with extremely subtle material depth and fine edge highlights. On the tile, two equally sized warm ivory paper panels, side by side with a slim teal gap, aligned, symmetric and equally emphasized. Each paper has three short engraved teal horizontal text strokes, identical in weight, with the bottom stroke slightly shorter; a tiny understated comparison notch between the panels may join their visual rhythm. Restrained premium desktop utility aesthetic, simple sculpted geometry, soft realistic shadows within the icon, crisp silhouette legible at 32 pixels. No letters, no words, no A or B, no checkmark favoring one side, no pen, no badges, no extra objects, no framing or scene. The only image is the icon. Background outside the rounded tile genuinely transparent. Keep tile around 82 percent of image width with rounded corners matching a contemporary macOS app icon.

The retained source is 1254×1254 with alpha. Tauri's icon conversion supplies the native resolutions without changing the composition.
