01 / Generation

Cheap only helps if the label is right.

Every model receives the same briefs and produces its own input–target pairs. The common bar requires structural validity and PASS from both Sonnet and Opus.

12 categories · 8 briefs each

Where the differences appear

Counts passing the common bar out of 8 requested briefs in each category. These small samples can reveal problems, but cannot establish equivalence.

Length, repetition, and structural failures

Distinct trigrams and near-duplicates describe this sample, not semantic quality or diversity at 30,000 examples. Length adherence is reported separately from text-check acceptance. Structural failures include annotation or normalization mismatches and do not by themselves establish a meaning-changing target.

02 / Verification

The checker can become the expensive part.

All three models assess every returned label in randomized order with opaque IDs. They see the text and contract, without generator names or author notes.
